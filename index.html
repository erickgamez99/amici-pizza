Our Story

Where everyone's family — if only for an hour.

We named the place Amici because that's what happens inside. Strangers at tables next to each other start talking about the pie they just ordered. Kids who came in crying leave holding a cannoli and laughing. Regulars walk in and the line cook already knows what they want.

We grew up in pizzerias back East — the kind where you could hear the dough slap the counter from the parking lot. When we moved to Arizona, we couldn't find one that tasted like home. So we built it. Slow-fermented dough. San Marzano sauce. Mozzarella we shred every morning. Everything made from scratch, because the shortcut is where the love gets lost.

Whether you're grabbing a slice after soccer practice or sitting down for a family dinner — we're glad you came. Mangia, amici.

— The Amici family
